Data Curation · 4-Source Integration Architecture

From 3,874 Raw Records to 3,360 Reconciled Mainshocks Every cleaning decision addresses a specific distortion mechanism before econometric modelling.

1. USGS ComCat

3,874 raw M6.0+ shocks collapsed via 150 km & ±2-day Haversine greedy clustering into 3,360 mainshocks. Flagged 788 (23.5%) fixed 10 km / 33 km placeholders.

2. NOAA + Wikipedia

Rescued 2006–2013 bullet-point years & parsed text range midpoints. Reconciled 379 deadly mainshocks (106 disagreements, log r = 0.993).

3. World Bank WDI

Joined constant 2021 PPP GDP per capita & historical income tiers by (country_iso3, year) across 1,905 onshore mainshocks (95.9% match).

4. Missingness Audit

1,310 offshore quakes separated structurally; 49 onshore quakes (Taiwan, Venezuela, New Caledonia) explicitly labelled as MAR.
